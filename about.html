Why Travel?

Because Travel Gives Us Something to Look Forward To

Travel has a way of bringing people together. It can be the family trip everyone talks about for years, the anniversary you finally take, the cruise you’ve always wanted to try, or the faraway place you never thought you would actually see.

That is what I love about this work. I get to hear the dream first. Then I get to help turn it into something real.

I’ve traveled myself, including time in Ireland, and every trip reminds me how much the little details matter. Where you stay, how much running around is involved, what you want to see, and what you would rather skip can completely change how a vacation feels.

A Little More About Me

I’m Happiest When I’m Helping Someone Else Get Excited About Going Somewhere

Some people love planning vacations because they love spreadsheets. I love it because I love people. I enjoy hearing what would make someone happy, learning what matters to them, and then digging into the possibilities until we find the right fit.

Sometimes that means a Disney vacation. Sometimes it means a cruise. Sometimes it means Ireland, a resort, an anniversary getaway, a group trip, or somewhere you have been dreaming about for years.
